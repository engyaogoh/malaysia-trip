Why this shape: Scoot flies direct Singapore → Ipoh (currently listed as TR486, departing SIN mid-afternoon on 16 Dec — verify the exact date and seats when booking), and home is a direct Penang → Singapore flight. KL and Johor are left out: every extra city means another hotel check-in and another long ride. In Ipoh you'll have a private 7-seater MPV with driver on sightseeing days — the driver drops your parents at the door of every kopitiam and attraction. For the Ipoh → Penang transfer the MPV also beats the train: no platforms, no luggage-hauling, just door-to-door with a toilet stop on demand.

Day 2 · Thu 17 Dec

Old Town at walking pace

photo: Wikimedia Commons
  • 8:30 breakfast: Food Yum Cha House or Foh San — Ipoh-style dim sum, trolleys come to you. go before 10am
  • 10:00: Sight Han Chin Pet Soo — a 1929 tin-mining club turned air-conditioned museum. closed Mondays ✓ · free · book ahead
  • 11:45: Food Sin Yoon Loong white coffee (the 1937 pioneer) with egg tarts. daily 6:30am–2:30pm ✓
  • 12:30 lunch: Food Thean Chun coffee shop — kai see hor fun + caramel custard. morning–mid-afternoon, confirm locally
  • 2–5pm: Hotel rest. (Optional add-on: Ho Yan Hor Museum, Tue–Sun 10am–4pm, free.)
  • 5pm: Sight Flat stroll: Concubine Lane (liveliest late afternoon), the murals, photo stop at the white railway station — "Taj Mahal of Ipoh". Driver drops at each corner.

Day 3 · Fri 18 Dec

Mirror-lake morning, long lunch, dessert

photo: Wikimedia Commons
  • 9:30: Sight Tasik Cermin (Mirror Lake) — limestone cliffs reflected in still green water, optional boat ride through the rock corridor. Viewing the lake is easy; the boat needs one careful step in/out. open daily 9am–5:45pm · boats 9am–5:30pm · online ticket ≥1 day ahead, non-refundable
  • 12:30 lunch: Food Cowan Street Chicken by Ann Xin — famous bean-sprout chicken rice. closed Thursdays → we go Friday ✓
  • 4:30 dessert: Food Funny Mountain tau fu fah — silky soya-bean pudding, warm with syrup. afternoon, check hours locally
  • En route: Heong Peng biscuits from a Gunung Rapat bakery — the classic Ipoh gift. Rain backup: skip the boat, the lake view is free.

Kellie's Castle and a slow afternoon

  • Dinner: Food Cowan Street Ayam Tauge & Koitiau — locals' favourite bean-sprout chicken with braised chicken feet. go early, before the weekend crowd

Tips for Travelling with Parents

  • One anchor per day. Each day has exactly one main outing plus meals — swap, never add.
  • No temples anywhere in this plan. Every stop is heritage, nature or food: museums, mansions, a lake, a hill, markets and coffeeshops.
  • December weather: west coast is mostly dry but a shower can appear; pack umbrellas, outdoors in the morning, rest 1–3pm.
  • Hotels: lifts, walk-in showers, restaurant downstairs, easy car drop-off. Two rooms close together if separate.
  • Book ahead (Dec is Malaysian school-holiday peak): flights, hotels, MPV driver, Tasik Cermin online tickets (≥1 day before), Blue Mansion 11am tour, Penang Hill fast-lane. Han Chin Pet Soo also needs advance booking.
  • Opening-hours cheat-sheet: kopitiams peak early and close by mid-afternoon (Sin Yoon Loong shuts 2:30pm); hawkers peak at night (Chulia St from 4:30pm); Ann Xin closed Thu; The TOP shorter Tue; Blue Mansion tours only 11am/3:30pm. Every listing above carries its hours — reconfirm by phone/Google a day before.
  • Money: hawkers are cash — carry small RM notes (~RM100/day between you). Grab takes card.
  • Health: regular meds in hand luggage; consider airport wheelchair assistance if terminal walks tire them. Travel insurance: check age limits for 70+.
