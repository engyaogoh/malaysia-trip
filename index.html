Why no KL or Johor: both mean extra hotel moves and long car legs. With Dad (73) and Mum (71) on board, two bases with everything walkable or a 10-min Grab away beats ticking a third city. Scoot flies direct Singapore → Ipoh, so you land right where the food is, and fly home direct Penang → Singapore — the only driving is a single 30-min bridge hop from the train.

Day by Day

Day 1 · Tue 16 Dec

Fly in, eat early, sleep well

  • Morning: Scoot direct flight Singapore → Ipoh (~1h 20m). Land, Grab (~15 min) to hotel in Ipoh Old Town / Tin Town area.
  • Afternoon: Check in, nap. Walk only as far as the coffee shop.
  • Dinner: Food Lou Wong or Onn Kee — Ipoh taugeh ayam (bean sprouts chicken) with silky sar hor fun. Ipoh's crunchy fat bean sprouts are the city's signature.
✈️ Scoot SIN–IPH flights

Pace: travel day — one meal out, nothing else.

Day 2 · Wed 17 Dec

Old Town at walking pace

  • Breakfast: Food Sin Yoon Loong (since 1937, the original white coffee) or Nam Heong next door — white coffee, egg tarts, kai see hor fun. Sit down; no queues to chase.
  • Morning: Sight Flat, shaded stroll through Ipoh Old Town: Concubine Lane, Ernest Zacharevic murals, the colonial railway station ("Taj Mahal of Ipoh").
  • Lunch: Food Old Town curry mee or Thean Chun coffee shop (chicken intestines optional 😄, caramel custard mandatory).
  • Afternoon: Sight Ho Yan Hor Museum — small, air-conditioned, free, all on one level. Perfect for parents.
  • Dinner: Salt-baked chicken (Aun Kheng Lim) + simple wantan mee.

Pace: ~2 km total walking, all flat, with a long afternoon break.

Day 3 · Thu 18 Dec

Cave temple morning, long lunch, rest

  • Morning: Sight Kek Lok Tong cave temple & zen gardens (15 min Grab). Drive right up to the entrance; the cave is cool and mostly level, with a pretty garden behind.
  • Lunch: Food Moon De Moon or Pasir Pinji big-tree-head yong tau foo / chee cheong fun nearby.
  • Afternoon: Home for nap. Optional: Ipoh pomelo from Tambun on the way back — sweet, juicy, travels home well.
  • Dinner: Food Freshwater prawns or Tuck Kee roast delights — or just repeat yesterday's favourites. That's allowed on holiday.

Pace: one attraction, one long lunch, one nap. Peak relaxation.

Day 4 · Fri 19 Dec

Dim sum morning, cave temple, slow afternoon

  • Breakfast: Food Foh San dim sum — Ipoh's grand old dim sum hall. Go before 9am for the trolleys; everything comes to you, no standing.
  • Morning: Sight Perak Tong cave temple — magnificent golden Buddha inside a limestone cave. View the cave hall from the entrance; only climb the 400+ steps if the knees volunteer.
  • Afternoon: Free time — massage, hotel pool, or the Ipoh Heritage Trail café-hopping (Sin Yoon Loong round two).
  • Dinner: Food Yuk Sou Hin (Weil Hotel, proper Cantonese) or another taugeh ayam night — it's that good.

Pace: two soft stops, everything drive-up.

Day 5 · Sat 20 Dec

The scenic train to Penang

  • Morning: Late breakfast, 10am checkout. Sight ETS train Ipoh → Butterworth, ~2h 15m of comfortable, air-con, scenic Kinta Valley riding. Book seats together early.
  • Afternoon: Grab/taxi over the Penang bridge (~30–40 min) to a hotel in George Town (heritage area) — pick one with a lift and a café downstairs.
  • Evening: Food Gurney Drive hawker centre — asam laksa, char koay teow, oyster omelette, muar chee. Order one plate at a time; wander between stalls as appetite allows.

Day 6 · Sun 21 Dec

George Town heritage & street food

  • Morning: Sight Chew Jetty (boardwalk, flat) → Khoo Kongsi clan house → Pinang Peranakan Mansion (chairs inside, air-con, Mum will love it).
  • Lunch: Food Penang Road Teochew chendul + kim tam kway teow th'ng nearby.
  • Afternoon: Sight Ernest Zacharevic street art hunt — or hire a trishaw; the ones in George Town take adults comfortably and Dad steers from the bench.
  • Dinner: Food Nyonya cuisine (Aunt Gaik or Nyonya Breeze) — book ahead, Christmas week is busy.

Day 7 · Mon 22 Dec

Up the hill, into the temple — both by cable

  • Morning: Sight Penang Hill funicular — a proper train to the top, cool air and island views. Book the fast-lane ticket online; no climbing needed.
  • Lunch: Food Air Itam asam laksa at the market — the most famous laksa in Malaysia, 10 min from the hill's base.
  • Afternoon: Sight Kek Lok Si temple — Malaysia's largest Buddhist temple. The incline lift carries you up to the Kuan Yin statue; the main halls are reachable with minimal stairs.
  • Evening: Early dinner near the hotel; Christmas-eve-eve rest.

Day 8 · Tue 23 Dec

Sea breeze and a farewell feast

  • Morning: Sight Grab to Batu Ferringhi — seaside breakfast, a short beach stroll, or just sit with coconut water. Optional: Tropical Spice Garden (mostly shaded, gentle paths).
  • Afternoon: Last-minute shopping — white coffee packets, pomelos, Heong Peah biscuits from Him Heang / Empress for the family. Or pack; check-in luggage still pays off.
  • Dinner: Food Farewell Chinese seafood dinner — something with butter prawns and steamed fish. Toast the trip.

Home for Christmas Eve

Tips for Travelling with Parents

  • One anchor per day. Every day above has exactly one main outing plus meals — swap, never add.
  • December weather: west-coast Malaysia is mostly dry but expect a shower or two; carry a compact umbrella. Start mornings early, rest 1–3pm, eat dinner at 7.
  • Hotels: pick lifts-not-stairs, walk-in showers, and locations where the famous coffeeshop is downstairs. In Ipoh: old town / Weil Hotel. In Penang: George Town heritage core.
  • Book now-ish: Scoot flights, ETS seats (sell out for school holidays), and Nyonya dinner. Everything else can be decided on the day.
  • Money: hawkers are cash — small RM notes. Grab takes card. Bring ~RM100/day cash between you.
  • Health: bring regular meds in hand luggage, note that Penang General Hospital and Ipoh's KPJ are reputable if ever needed (touch wood).
  • Christmas Eve flight: arriving home 24 Dec afternoon keeps Christmas intact — no one misses the family dinner.
